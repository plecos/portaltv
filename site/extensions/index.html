Extensions

Extensions are separate apps that add things to Portal: a tile, a button in the top bar, a settings page, an animated background, reminders, or where the weather comes from. Most are made by other people; Portal TV Weather, which comes with Portal, is made by Portal. You find them on your TV under Settings, Extensions, Browse extensions. This page shows the same list.

Extensions made by other people are not covered by Portal's no-ads, no-tracking promise. They are third-party apps. A listing means someone read the entry and checked its source link; it is not a security audit. Nothing an extension asks for happens until an adult allows it, and kid profiles get no extensions unless a parent says so.

Add yours

Write an extension in any language: it is an ordinary Android TV app with a small manifest. Start with the quickstart, then read the full contract. To be listed here and in Portal's Browse page, send a pull request to the catalogue repository. The review checklist is in its README.

What this page sends

Your browser fetches one file, catalog.json, and the small pictures it names, all from this site. Nothing else is requested, and the page keeps no record of you. Portal on your TV checks the file's signature before it reads it; this page does not, so treat it as a preview.
